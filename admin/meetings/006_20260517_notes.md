## Notes
### Summary of Survey Suggestions ("Sprint 2")
We'll rate the questions out of 5 (1 = not really important and/or veering towards offtopic, 5 = important in concept)

#### Patrick's Questions
1. iClicker/Top Hat frustrations - 2
   1. Not sure how many people would have used Top Hat. The frustrations part is interesting, but too vague?
2. Google OAuth - 4
   1. Either way, the students will have to login and/or identify themselves somehow anyway.
3. Device used during lecture - 5
4. Ease of drawing on device - 4
   1. This is a 4 mainly because drawing probably isn't going to be a very common question, but it could be interesting to get info on this.
5. Draw on blank or annotate over image - 1
   1. This is dependent on the lecture question that the professor wants to ask, so there isn't a point in getting student input.
6. Likelihood of participating given names publically displayed - 2
   1. Students have to participate regardless if they want their participation points. We can also add a anonymize responses feature.
7. Student answer confidence - 3
   1. The idea could be better if this was more like a "student answer distribution." Additionally, it's the professor's choice to show the distribution or not.
8. Gamification - 1
   1. Points and games isn't really the point of lecture. This is supposed to be a quick check-in.
9. "Checked-in today" status - 1
   1.  This isn't really something you should be asking a student as they may use it to just check in and dip lecture. Additionally, it might be better to have a "your answer has been submitted" for each question as confirmation.
